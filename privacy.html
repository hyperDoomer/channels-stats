Privacy policy

Channels Stats is used only by its owner for channels the owner runs or manages with the channel holder's consent.

What it reads. With read-only access granted through Google sign-in, it reads YouTube channel and video statistics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, subscribers gained) via the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

What it does with it. The statistics are stored privately for the owner's own reporting. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored statistics are deleted on request.

Channels Stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
